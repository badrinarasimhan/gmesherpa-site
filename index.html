The challenge

The good gets forgotten.

Real development happens daily — in a hallway, after a code, mid-rounds. But in today's systems, recording a win is as onerous as recording a concern. So concerns get documented, wins evaporate, and oversight only ever sees the problems.

Concerns get documented

Compliance forces the issue.

Defensibility means the negative is always preserved — and, being negative, it lingers on the record long after the moment has passed.

Growth gets forgotten

No forcing function.

Too much friction, and nothing that compels it. The wins fade — by the next committee meeting, the good is simply gone.

captured · rarely

Security & privacy

Built for a record that cannot be casual.

Sherpa handles assessments of named physicians in training. The controls below are engineering facts about how the system is built and enforced, not aspirations.

Where data lives

Pinned region, asserted at deploy

Database region and model-processing locations are fixed per environment and recorded as one immutable residency set. A deployment fails if that trio is missing or inconsistent — the check runs in the pipeline, not in a policy document.

Which services may touch it

A vendor sweep that blocks the build

Every cloud service the platform enables must carry a dated, classified entry in a service manifest stamped from the provider's own covered-products list. The estate cannot silently gain a service: continuous integration fails on any gap.

Who can read what

Isolation per institution

Each institution is a separate identity tenant, and every record read is scoped to the institution that owns it. Sessions are server-side; the browser holds an opaque reference over an encrypted connection, restricted to the paths that need it.

What leaves the system

Nothing sensitive on a lock screen

Mobile notifications carry an opaque reference and never clinical content — a phone screen is not a private place. Notifications are also deliberately rare: routine progress does not interrupt anyone's day.

Least privilege

Migrations run without superuser rights

Schema changes execute under a restricted role, so a change that would only succeed with elevated privileges fails in rehearsal rather than in production.

Human ratification

Nothing is filed automatically

Analysis proposes; a person approves. Every assessment that reaches a trainee’s record has been read and signed by a faculty member, and the official system of record remains the source of truth.

Business Associate Agreements and institutional review. A Business Associate Agreement is in place with our cloud provider, and every service the platform enables is drawn from that provider’s BAA-covered list — enforced by the manifest check described above, which fails the build on any gap. Sherpa is designed to operate under a BAA with each partner institution. Security questionnaires, architecture review and pilot-stage due diligence are welcome — write to security@gmesherpa.com.
Privacy policy

What we collect, and why.

Last updated 2 August 2026. This policy covers gmesherpa.com and the Sherpa GME platform, including our mobile applications.

Who we are

The controller of this data

Math Gambit LLC, 835 N Webster St, Naperville, IL 60563, United States. Questions about this policy: privacy@gmesherpa.com.

On behalf of institutions

Most data is not ours to decide about

Assessment records are held for a sponsoring institution under written agreement. The institution decides what is collected, who may see it and how long it is kept; we process it under their instruction and do not use it for our own purposes.

What we collect

Account, assessment and technical data

Names, institutional email addresses and programme roles for people the institution enrols; assessments, learning plans and related notes created by faculty; and technical records — sign-in times, device identifiers used to deliver notifications, and error logs needed to keep the service working.

What we never do

No sale, no advertising, no training on your record

We do not sell personal information and we serve no advertising. Assessment content is not used to train general-purpose models: our model-processing terms exclude submitted content from training, and no assessment leaves the processing region pinned for your institution.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion

Requests about an assessment record should go to your institution, which controls it. For anything we hold directly, write to privacy@gmesherpa.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Retention and sub-processors

Kept as agreed, processed where stated

Records are retained for the period set in the institutional agreement and deleted or returned at its end. Hosting and model processing run on Google Cloud in a pinned region. A current sub-processor list is available on request.

Questions about this policy should go to privacy@gmesherpa.com. If your institution has a Business Associate Agreement with us, that agreement governs how we handle protected health information and prevails over this page wherever the two differ.
